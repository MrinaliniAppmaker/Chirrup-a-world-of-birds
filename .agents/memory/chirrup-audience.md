name: Chirrup mixed-age audience
description: Intended child and adult audience and the limits of release-readiness claims
---
Describe Chirrup's intended audience as **“for all ages”**. This includes children under 13; do not silently narrow it to a teen-only or adult-only app.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “Both age groups” during production preparation, then approved the release plan and requested the wording “for all ages”.

**How to apply:** Treat child-access controls, appropriate guardian consent, sharing safety, SDK eligibility and AI-provider permissions as release requirements. A privacy-policy paragraph or a parent-owned account is not evidence that those requirements are met. Verify the agreement applicable to Replit-managed AI access rather than assuming the direct provider's terms either apply unchanged or are waived. Do not change the audience, providers, generation models or prices without approval.
